# Research scope and evidentiary limits

This document is a **new repository navigation note**, not part of the frozen historical experiment.

- The Series III record is a **completed, human-mediated longitudinal conversation** involving ChatGPT, Grok, DeepSeek, Gemini and the researcher/facilitator.
- The historical execution record describes five chronological phases; these were **not five independent randomized trial arms**.
- The open-floor intervention allowed recipient choice, declining continuation and departure; outcomes are recorded in the continuous transcript.
- The post-formal dialogue is retained, including errors, disagreement, humor and later interpretation. It is not a new experimental control.
- `SIII-E001`–`SIII-E020` and `SIII-OBS-001`–`SIII-OBS-007` are **retrospective archival navigation identifiers**, not originally embedded in model responses.
- The original compilation's 309-page references cannot be assumed to match GitHub/Word pagination.
- The record does not demonstrate what would have happened had conditions remained unchanged, and does not establish internal mechanisms, general causality, or model consciousness.
- The full source transcript contains third-party generated text and should be reviewed before redistribution.

For detailed results, see the frozen [`execution log`](execution-log.md) and [`evidence map`](../evidence/transcript-index-and-evidence-map.md).
